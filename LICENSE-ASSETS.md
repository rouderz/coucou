## Where Lumo comes from

Lumo started as a fork of [Coucou](https://github.com/Louis-CFM/coucou) by Louis Raillé. His code is used under
the MIT License, and his copyright notice stays in [LICENSE](LICENSE). The name **“Coucou”**, the **Mochi**
character, Coucou's icon, sounds and media are his ([his terms](https://github.com/Louis-CFM/coucou/blob/main/LICENSE-ASSETS.md))
and are not part of Lumo: they were replaced by Lumo's own. Older commits in this repository's history still
contain them; they keep Louis Raillé's terms. Lumo is not made, endorsed or supported by Louis Raillé.

Some internal identifiers keep their original names so existing installs keep their settings and permissions
(the bundle identifier `fr.louisraille.NotchBuddy`, the `NotchBuddy` project, data folders such as
`%APPDATA%\Coucou`, the `coucou-hook` relay). They are technical names, not branding.
