# Bao, Pip, Nube, Lumo and Tecla — characters

Copyright (c) 2026 Wolfgang Welcomez (rouderz).

The five characters added in this fork — **Bao** (a steamed bun), **Pip** (a sprout), **Nube** (a little
cloud), **Lumo** (a jellyfish) and **Tecla** (a keyboard key) — are original designs made for this fork.
They are not based on Mochi or on any existing character.

## What this covers

- the characters' **names** as characters (Bao, Pip, Nube, Lumo, Tecla);
- their **design and look**: silhouettes, colour palettes, faces (eyes, brows, eye shine, mouths, blush),
  accessories (pleats, leaf, puffs, tentacles, key top) and their expressions and animations as characters.

The **code** that draws them — `NotchBuddy/Sources/App/Bot/Critter.swift` and `windows/src/mochi/critters.ts`,
among others — is part of the source code and stays under the [MIT License](LICENSE).
